Pico sounds robotic, so the plan stays: pre-made audio (e.g. Polly) for questions, Pico as the offline fallback. Note for M1: expo-speech uses the default engine, so we need the engine choice and the `<queries>` entry via a config plugin. |
| S1 | UsageStats sees Netflix/Prime/Disney+ in front | ✅ pass | 2026-10-05 | Detected 0.25–1.1 s after the app came to the front (Netflix `com.netflix.ninja`, Prime Video `com.amazon.firebat`, Disney+ `com.disney.disneyplus`; no YouTube on this stick). **Found:** the screensaver (`com.amazon.ftv.screensaver`) must be on the free list, or the jar drains while nobody watches; added together with `com.amazon.tv.ftvambient`. |
| S2 | LockActivity comes to the front over those apps | ✅ pass | 2026-10-05 | With the jar empty, the lock covered each app 0.5–1.1 s after it opened. Prime Video playback also stopped behind the lock (accessibility watchdog on, 90 s jar; confirmed by eye). A real Netflix episode still to try. |
| S3 | Home → reopen; "Alexa, open Netflix" | ✅ pass | 2026-10-05 | Both lock again (0.25 s and 1.2 s). |
| S4 | Survives a reboot and 2 h idle | ✅ with the watchdog | 2026-10-05 | **Without** the accessibility watchdog: after `adb reboot` the stick was reachable in ~25 s, but `BOOT_COMPLETED` reached our receiver only ~90 s after boot (Fire OS delivers it to its own apps first), so the watcher was back at ~90 s, not ≤ 60 s. **With** the watchdog: the system binds enabled accessibility services early, so the watcher was back **~28 s after boot** ✅. **2 h idle ✅:** at 13:02 the watcher was the same process (pid 7690), still logging a heartbeat every minute while the TV was dreaming (screensaver, allowlisted, so no drain). The stick was also used for ~50 min of MarbleTime testing in between, which it metered correctly (jar 36000 s → 33068 s). |
| S5 | Prime Video controls with accessibility on | ✅ pass | 2026-10-05 | With our accessibility service enabled, Prime Video's playback controls still hid by themselves (confirmed by eye). So the watchdog can be on by default, unless later tests show otherwise. |
| S6 | Force-stop → heartbeat; kid profile blocks Settings; local ADB | ⚠️ findings | 2026-10-05 | **Force stop** (Settings → Applications) kills the watcher, **removes our accessibility service from the enabled list** and leaves the app `stopped=true`, so nothing restarts it, not even a reboot, until a parent re-runs the ADB grant. So the heartbeat → "TV not reporting" alert is essential, and Settings must be out of reach (kid profile: part of your K checks). **Local ADB:** adbd listens on all interfaces, and a TCP connection to `127.0.0.1:5555` from the stick itself works, so an on-device setup helper looks possible (key authorisation not tested yet). |
